Kin for Windows, Android and Linux.

## Download

[**v0.0.7 Stable**](https://github.com/ozimellow/Kin-Client/releases/tag/v0.0.7) is available for Windows x64, Android ARM64 (Android 10 or later) and Linux x64 desktop.

**Windows:** download the ZIP, extract the complete folder and open the application.

**Android:** download the APK and follow Android's installation prompts.

**Linux:** download the ZIP, extract the complete folder and open the application.

Add your connection token to get started.

## Updates

Open **Settings > About** to check for updates. Windows, Android and Linux require version **0.0.7 or later** for new connections.

## Support and licence

[Report a problem](https://github.com/ozimellow/Kin-Client/issues) with your version and operating system. Do not share connection tokens or credentials.
